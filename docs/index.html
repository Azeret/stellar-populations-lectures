Think about it

1. Did your 30 stars contain a supernova progenitor? How many pairs in the room got one?

2. Press +10 000 a few times. Does the histogram now follow the line? Where does it still scatter, and why?

3. Most stars are small. Which stars carry most of the mass? Which make most of the light?

Galaxy chef: the [Mg/Fe] clock

You have 8 units of star formation to spend over 8 rounds (1 round ≈ 250 Myr).

Each unit makes 3 Mg + 1 Fe at once (core-collapse SN II). It also books iron from Type Ia SNe: +1 Fe one round later, +1 two rounds later and +1 four rounds later.

Stars born in a round inherit the pot's [Mg/Fe] at that moment. The customer gets the mass-weighted [Mg/Fe] of all your stars.

The rules in numbers

[Mg/Fe] = log₁₀(Mg/Fe) relative to a 1:1 pot, which we call solar. Pure SN II gives Mg/Fe = 3, i.e. [Mg/Fe] = +0.48.
